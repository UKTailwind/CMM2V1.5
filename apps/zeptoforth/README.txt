zeptoforth (Generation 1 and 2)
-------------------------------
  CHDIR "A:/zeptoforth"
  RUN "zeptoforth.app"           on a Generation 2
  RUN "zeptoforth_g1.app"        on a Generation 1

Travis Bemann's Forth, with preemptive tasks, double precision, the editor
zeptoed, and words for the screen, sound, files and I/O pins. Type bye to go
back to MMBasic. Ctrl-C stops a word that is running and restarts Forth.

WORDS.md lists the Colour Maximite 2's words. It needs firmware 7.00.00RC3 or
later. zeptoforth.img and zeptoforth_g1.img are the saved Forth systems: keep
each with its program. zeptoforth is under the MIT licence, and its console has
libvterm (MIT) and the Spleen fonts (BSD 2-Clause).
