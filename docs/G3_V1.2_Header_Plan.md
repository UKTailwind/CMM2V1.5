# Colour Maximite 2 G3 V1.2: the on-board Wi-Fi module and an unchanged connector

*22 November 2026. Written from Peter's schematic "CMM2 V2 G3 V1.2" (updated 18 November), compared with the G3 V1.1
(15 November), the CMM2 Wifi V1.1 add-on (16 November) and the STM32H743 datasheet (DS12110 Rev 4). The firmware
references are to v8-dev at 62a6e5b. Peter chose this layout, option A below, on 22 November and made the board
changes the same day. His revised V1.2 schematic (22 November) was checked against the tables below: every connection
matches. His Gerbers of 22 November (15:02) were then checked for the connections and the antenna keep-out, below. No
V1.2 board has been built yet. The firmware below was written on 22 November and tested on a G3 V1.1 as far as a
board without the module allows.*

## The decision

V1.2 puts the RM2 Wi-Fi and Bluetooth module on the board. Peter's first V1.2 schematic kept the module on the
processor pins the add-on uses (PB13, PB15, PI3, PI8) and gave their connector places to PF6, PF7, PF8, PG13 and PG14.
That would have changed the connector table on page 24 of the manual.

**Option A, chosen:** the connector keeps exactly the processor pins of a G2 and a G3 V1.1, so page 24 stays true as
it is. The module moves to the pins nothing uses: SPI6 on PG13 and PG14, chip select on PF7, power-on on PF8. A strap
on PF6 tells the firmware the module is on the board.

- **Page 24 still holds:** SPI2 with 4 to 32-bit transfers on pins 40, 38 and 35, CAN2 TX on pin 40, the general
  I/O on pins 32 and 33, the 5V marks, and the factory reset on pin 40.
- **SPI2 and Wi-Fi can be used at the same time on a V1.2.** On a G2 with the add-on they share SPI2 and cannot.
- **Firmware already released still runs a V1.2 as a G3,** with everything except the on-board Wi-Fi (below).

**Option B, rejected:** BASIC's SPI2 on SPI5 at PF7, PF9 and PF8. It would have matched the pin numbers only:
- SPI5 is one of the H743's limited SPIs, with frames of at most 16 bits. The HAL treats only SPI1-3 as able to go
  above that. BASIC's SPI allows 4 to 32 bits today.
- CAN2 would have been lost.
- Pins 35, 38 and 40 would have lost their 5V marks, by the manual's rule for analog-capable pins.
- PF9, the G1/G2 strap, would have moved to the connector, so no released firmware could have started a V1.2.

Option B's extras, three more analog inputs and two more PWM outputs, are not in option A.

## Board connections

U13 is the STM32H743IIT6 (LQFP176). The MMBasic pin number is 41 minus the P1 number.

### The RM2 module (U1, RMC20452T)

| RM2 pin | RM2 name | Connect to |
|---|---|---|
| 1, 4, 7, 11, 15, 21 | Ground | GND |
| 2, 19, 20 | nc | not connected |
| 3 | gSPI_SCLK | **PG13** (U13 pin 156), SPI6 SCK |
| 5 | gSPI_Data_In | **PG14** (U13 pin 157), SPI6 MOSI, directly |
| 6 | gSPI_Data_out | **PG14**, through 470 ohms (R6) |
| 8 | GPIO0 | not connected |
| 9 | gSPICS | **PF7** (U13 pin 25) |
| 10 | nIRQ | **PG14**, through 10k (R4) |
| 12 | Wi-Fi_on | **PF8** (U13 pin 26) |
| 13 | Bluetooth_on | **PF8** (U13 pin 26) |
| 14 | VDDIO | VCCBAT (as now) |
| 16 | Vin | VCCBAT (as now) |
| 17, 18 | GPIO2, GPIO1 | not connected |

The module's supply stays as drawn: U2 (AMS1117-3.3) from +5V, then L4 to VCCBAT.

### The rest

| Net | Connect to | Note |
|---|---|---|
| PF6 (U13 pin 24) | GND | **New: the V1.2 strap** |
| PF9 (U13 pin 27) | GND | As now: the G1/G2 strap |
| P1 pin 1 (MMBasic 40) | PB13 (U13 pin 93) | As on V1.1 |
| P1 pin 3 (MMBasic 38) | PB15 (U13 pin 95) | As on V1.1 |
| P1 pin 6 (MMBasic 35) | PB14 (U13 pin 94) | As on V1.1 |
| P1 pin 8 (MMBasic 33) | PI3 (U13 pin 134) | As on V1.1 |
| P1 pin 9 (MMBasic 32) | PI8 (U13 pin 7) | As on V1.1 |

- **Remove** the module's connections to PB13, PB15, PI3 and PI8. Those pins go only to the connector, as on a G2.
- **R5, the add-on's 10k from the module's clock to VCC, is removed.** On the add-on it held pin 40 high for the
  factory-reset test. PG13 is not on the connector, so a V1.2 does not need it, and its VCC track ran through the
  antenna keep-out (below).
- **Choosing the pins:**
  - **Clock and data:** SPI6 is the only SPI whose clock and data can both reach unused pins (PG13 and PG14,
    alternate function 5) without taking PF9. SPI5's MOSI can only be on PF9, or on PF11, which is the SDRAM's.
  - **Not otherwise used:** the firmware's LTDC set-up uses PG6, PG7 and PG10-PG12, and the SDRAM uses PG0-PG2, PG4,
    PG5, PG8 and PG15. Nothing in the firmware touches PF6-PF8, PG13 or PG14.
  - **Chip select and power-on** can be any spare GPIO. If other pins route more easily than PF7 and PF8, they can
    change, and the firmware follows.

### The antenna keep-out

The RM2's antenna is at the end away from pins 8-14. Figure 6 of the
[RM2 datasheet](https://datasheets.raspberrypi.com/rm2/rm2-datasheet.pdf) gives an RF keep-out that must be free of
any metal on every layer:
- It is 35.5 mm wide, centred on the module (which is 14.5 mm wide).
- It starts 11.5 mm from the module's pin 8-14 edge, 0.7 mm past the centres of pins 1 and 21.
- It runs 18.5 mm towards the antenna: 5 mm over the module's antenna end and 13.5 mm beyond it.

Section 2.6 of the datasheet asks for a good ground plane under the rest of the module.

On the V1.2 (Gerber coordinates, mm):

| | Position |
|---|---|
| Module | x 113.58 (pin 8-14 edge) to 130.08 (antenna end), y 17.13 to 31.63; U1's placement point at y 24.38 |
| Board edge | x 130.06: the antenna end is flush with it, as it should be |
| Keep-out on the board | x 125.08 to the edge, y 6.63 to 42.13 |
| Keep-out off the board | 13.5 mm beyond the edge: no metal there either, such as a case wall |

The first V1.2 Gerbers cleared only the strip in line with the module. The GND (inner 1) and VCC (inner 2) planes
filled both sides of the keep-out, and C5 and R5's VCC track were inside it. In the final Gerbers:
- **Planes:** both inner planes are cut back from y 6.47 to 42.23 (inner 1) and 42.30 (inner 2). Both still reach
  x 124.8-125.0 under the module, up to the keep-out line.
- **C5** (U2's 10 µF on +5V) has moved to x 124.21. Its pads end on the keep-out line.
- **R5** is removed, with its track.
- **Top and bottom layers** carry no other copper in the keep-out.

### Checked on the Gerbers

Peter's Gerbers of 22 November (15:02, with the BOM and pick-and-place files of 14:46) were checked:
- **Method:** the four copper layers were rasterised at 30 pixels per mm, and the copper islands were joined through
  the plated holes. The result was compared with the net list in the zip (FlyingProbeTesting.json). A deliberately
  wrong net in a copy was caught as both an open and a short.
- **Connections:** all 233 nets connect as drawn, with no opens and no shorts.
- **The module's nets:**
  - PG13 runs to RM2 pin 3 only.
  - PG14 runs to RM2 pin 5, to R6 and to R4.
  - PF7 runs to pin 9, and PF8 to pins 12 and 13.
  - PF6 and PF9 go to GND.
  - Connector pins 1, 3, 6, 8 and 9 go only to PB13, PB15, PB14, PI3 and PI8.
- **Bus tracks:**
  - The clock and data tracks are about 100 mm each, through three vias. Chip select and power-on are about 70 mm.
  - On a G2, PB13 and PB15 already run about 95 mm to the connector, before the add-on's own wiring. So the on-board
    bus is the shorter one.
- **The keep-out:** met on all four layers, as above.

## How the firmware tells the boards apart

`getVersion()` ([Src/main.c](../Src/main.c)) reads PF9 and PF6 early in `main()`, before the clocks and the SDRAM
are set up. The MS7210's answer on the RTC's bus still tells a G3 from a G2.

| Board | PF9 | PF6 | Where the firmware looks for the RM2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | high | high | the add-on, on the connector |
| G2 | low | high | the add-on, on the connector |
| G3 V1.1 | low | high | the add-on, on the connector |
| **G3 V1.2** | low | **low** | **on the board: SPI6 on PG13/PG14, CS PF7, power-on PF8** |

- PF6 has no connection on a G1 (Peter, 22 November), a G2 V2.0 or a G3 V1.1.
- **Older firmware on a V1.2** (b2 and before): PF9 is low, so the board starts as a G2, and the MS7210 makes it a
  G3. The connector is as it expects. Wi-Fi looks for the add-on on the connector, finds no module, and says so.
- **An add-on plugged into a V1.2** is ignored. The firmware uses the module on the board, and the add-on's pins
  are never driven.

## Firmware changes

Written on v8-dev on 22 November. Everything new runs only on a V1.2: on every other board the code takes the
add-on's path, which is the code as it was.

1. **Board detection** (`getVersion()`, [Src/main.c](../Src/main.c)):
   - PF6 and PF9 are read together, with their pull-ups. `RM2OnBoard` (a new global in main.c, declared in
     Hardware_Includes.h) is set when both are low. A G1 is still told by PF9 alone.
   - Both pins are then left analog with no pull. The old code set GPIOE there instead of GPIOF, which left PF9's
     pull-up on (current into the strap) and reached for PE9 (SDRAM D6).
   - `RM2OnBoard` is in main.c's own data, so the network's re-initialised data (.radio) never clears it.
2. **The Wi-Fi bus** ([Src/net/cyw43_bus_cmm2.c](../Src/net/cyw43_bus_cmm2.c)), with `RM2OnBoard`:
   - **SPI6** on PG13 (clock) and PG14 (data), both AF5, medium speed, PG14 pulled down. Chip select is PF7. Half
     duplex, mode 0 and polled, as SPI2 is. RCC APB4 SPI6EN and SPI6RST.
   - **Clock:** the D3 bus clock (pclk4, SPI6SEL 0, its reset value), divided down to 30 MHz at most: 120 MHz / 4 =
     30 MHz on a 480 MHz board.
   - **Frames:** `spi16_send` and `spi16_receive` move each byte-reversed word as two 16-bit frames, high half
     first, one frame per 16-bit FIFO access. The bits on the wire are unchanged.
   - **Parts:** a transfer of more than 511 words (TSIZE: 1023 frames on a limited SPI) is made in parts with CS
     held low. The driver's largest transfers are 511 words, so in practice it is one part.
   - The gSPI framing below `make_cmd()`, pico-sdk's, is untouched.
3. **The module's pins** ([Src/net/cyw43_driver_cmm2.c](../Src/net/cyw43_driver_cmm2.c)): a second table,
   `pins_onboard`: WL_REG_ON PF8, WL_CS PF7, WL_HOST_WAKE PG14. The table is chosen by `RM2OnBoard` at each use.
4. **Reserved pins** ([Src/net/WebMite_cmm2.c](../Src/net/WebMite_cmm2.c)):
   - `reserve_pins()` returns at once on a V1.2, so pins 32, 33, 38 and 40 stay BASIC's.
   - With no module answering, `release_all()` turns SPI6 off and the module off (PF8 low), and leaves SPI2 and the
     four pins alone.
5. **BASIC's SPI2:** no change. SPI2 OPEN and CLOSE ([SPI.c](../Src/SPI.c)) only step aside for pins that are
   boot-reserved, so on a V1.2 SPI2 works with Wi-Fi as it does without it.
6. **Bluetooth** uses the same bus: no change.
7. **Banner and MM.INFO:** unchanged; a V1.2 is "Colour Maximite 2 G3".

Nothing changes in the pin table, CAN, PWM, the ADC or the factory reset. The network code is in its own flash
section, and the getVersion change moved no function in bank 1 (compared with `nm`), so nothing else's timing
changes.

### Tested without a V1.2 (22 November, the G3 V1.1 on COM50 and the G1 on COM51)

Two test builds tried the new code on the V1.1, then the real build was checked for regressions:

| Build | What it did | Result |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | The add-on driven by `spi16_send` and `spi16_receive` over SPI2 (16-bit frames, parts of 37 words to force many parts) | The chip's 225 KB firmware loaded and the board joined. 100 pings of 1400 bytes: all answered. A 1 MB TFTP round trip: identical. Bus errors: 0. |
| T2 | `RM2OnBoard` forced on: the V1.2 path on SPI6, with no module on PG13/PG14 | "WiFi module not found", prompt 2.2 s after a restart. Bus errors 0 (SPI6's transfers all ended, none timed out). SPI6 CFG1 1000000F: 16-bit frames, /4 = 30 MHz. PG13 AF5, PG14 AF5 input pulled down, PF7 and PF8 outputs. Pins 32, 33, 38 and 40 free; SPI2 OPEN and CLOSE and SETPIN 32 and 33 work. |
| Final | The add-on as before | Starts as the G3, options unchanged. Joined; pins 32, 33, 38 and 40 reserved; SPI2 OPEN refused ("Pin 38 is reserved on startup"). 100 pings answered, 1 MB TFTP identical. SD TIMING FAST still reads and writes 1 MB correctly. |
| Final, on the G1 (COM51) | The add-on as before, on a G1 | Starts as a G1 at 400 MHz, options unchanged, 4286K of heap free. Joined; pins reserved; SPI2 OPEN refused. 100 pings answered, 1 MB TFTP identical. SD TIMING FAST reads and writes 1 MB correctly. |

What only a V1.2 can test: the module's answers on SPI6 at 30 MHz over the board's tracks, Bluetooth there, and the
antenna.

## Manual changes

- **Page 24 (the connector table):** no change.
- **The G3 section of the hardware chapter:** a V1.2 has the Wi-Fi and Bluetooth module on the board.
- **Wi-Fi chapter:** on a G3 V1.2 the module uses no connector pins, so pins 32, 33, 38 and 40 stay free, and SPI2
  can be used with Wi-Fi.
- **SPI appendix:** the same note about SPI2 and Wi-Fi on a V1.2.

## Tests

On the first V1.2:

- **Detection:** it starts as a G3 with the module found on the board.
- **Wi-Fi on SPI6:** WEB CONNECT, the web server, TFTP and HTTPS. Note the bus speed it settled on, and the error
  count.
- **Bluetooth:** COM8 and OPTION AUDIO BLUETOOTH.
- **The antenna:** next to a G2 with the add-on, in the same place, compare the dBm figures a Bluetooth scan reports
  for the same devices, and the Wi-Fi transfer speed.
- **SPI2 alongside Wi-Fi:** a loop-back from pin 38 to pin 35 at each speed and 8, 16 and 32 bits, with Wi-Fi
  connected.
- **The connector:**
  - CAN 2 on pins 19 and 40.
  - SETPIN on pins 32, 33, 38 and 40 with Wi-Fi on.
  - The factory reset from pin 40.
- **Older firmware:** b2 on the V1.2 starts as a G3, everything but Wi-Fi works, and OPTION WIFI reports no module.

Regression on a G1 (COM43), a G2 and the G3 V1.1 (COM50):

- Each still starts as the same board.
- Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on the add-on work as before.
- SPI2 is still refused while Wi-Fi has it.
