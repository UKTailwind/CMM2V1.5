# Audio tests

Programs that check PLAY on the board, written with the audio merge (docs/Audio_Review_and_Merge_Plan.md).

- `gen_audio.py OUTDIR` makes two-second tones (WAV, FLAC and MP3, mono and stereo), a one-second mono 44.1 kHz WAV for PLAY EFFECT, a 2.56 s module (`tiny.mod`), and `audiotest.bas`, which times each tone from PLAY to its completion interrupt (all should take about 2.1 s). It copies `a1test.bas` beside them. It needs numpy, soundfile and lameenc.
- `a1test.bas` checks the fixes of the first audio commit: TONE and TTS interrupts, a 53-file directory (20 played, one interrupt at the end), the playlist's directory forms, MM.INFO(TRACK), PLAY STOP not firing the interrupt, PLAY SOUND's checks and pausing, a failed TTS, files it cannot play, 96 kHz and 24-bit files, MOD, MODSAMPLE volume 0, EFFECT over MOD, and the modules that must load. Beyond A:/snd it uses the files on Peter's audio card: `A:/SOUNDS` (53 short WAVs), `A:/mp3`, `A:/fLAC`, `A:/MUSICS` and `A:/music` (modules), and in the root `Crash.wav`, `Bombora.wav`, `sr004-01-24192.wav`, `sr005-01-2496.wav`, `48.wav`, `F96.flac`, `Sample_BeeMoved_96kHz24bit.flac`, `test.flac` and the root's `.mod` files.
- `levels.bas` reads the DAC output registers while each source plays and prints the minimum, mean and maximum of each channel (all centred on 2000 since the decisions of 16 Oct), then checks PAUSE, PLAY TONE while paused and the MM.INFO(SOUND) strings. It needs the files in A:/snd and `A:/PTETRIS.MOD`; run it with `RUN "A:/snd/levels.bas"` after sending it.
- `a3test.bas` checks PLAY ARRAY (timing at 8000, 44000 and 100 samples a second, start and end, errors, the DAC level), PLAY SAMPLE and RELEASE (envelope level and the interrupt at the end), PLAY LOAD SOUND with type "U", and PLAY SOUND's string forms. It needs A:/snd/ts_wav.wav.
- `a4test.bas` checks BBC sound at the DAC: pitch (by counting the square's crossings), level, duration, the queues and MM.INFO(BBC BUFFER), an envelope, noise, PAUSE, errors, and notes queued just as the engine runs dry. It needs A:/snd/ts_wav.wav.
- `a5test.bas` checks PLAY MODFILE played once with an interrupt (`tiny.mod`, 2.56 s, made by gen_audio.py), both forms of its arguments, looping without one, MM.INFO(TRACK) for a module, and the module's and an EFFECT's interrupts apart. It needs `tiny.mod` and `ts_wav.wav` in A:/snd and `A:/PTETRIS.MOD`.
- `run_audio.py OUTDIR --put` sends the files to `A:/snd` and runs both programs (COM4 at 460800 unless `--port` and `--baud` say otherwise).

Results of 16 Oct 2026, after the first audio commit: every tone 2.1 to 2.2 s; the TONE of 500 ms fired at 504 ms; TTS fired; the 53-file directory played 20 tracks and fired once; PLAY STOP fired nothing; every module on the card loads except `TESTIT.MOD` (an Impulse Tracker file, "IMPM") and the corrupt `TESTXM.MOD` and `TESTS3M.MOD`, which are refused with "Not a MOD file it can play". hxcmod takes any file without a signature for a 15-sample Soundtracker module, so `test.flac` "plays" as a MOD.
