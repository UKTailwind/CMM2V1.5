# 09b a 100 KB block (all 420 lines of 238 characters) cut with F4, then pasted back with F5: the clipboard grows to hold it
known the engine's clipboard is 1 KB (MAXCLIP: "MARKED TEXT EXCEEDS BUFFER SIZE"); E4 makes it grow, and puts its size on the status bar (kilo's grew in the internal heap, 256 KB at most, and stopped with "Not enough internal memory")
put t.bas big9.bas
cmd EDIT "t.bas"
status 420 lines
key f4
key down 419
key end
key f4
status 1 lines
status Paste Buffer 100800
key f5
status 421 lines
key f1
prompt
get t.bas expect9b.bas
